About the Registry & Data Sources

This platform aggregates official public holidays and observances across four critical layers with automated advance alert services:

  • Nigeria Layer: Statutory public holidays verified against the Public Holidays Act and Federal Ministry of Interior official gazettes.
  • Africa Layer: Continental commemorative days declared by the African Union Assembly (au.int) celebrating African heritage and Agenda 2063.
  • Power & Energy Sector Layer: Clean energy transitions, off-grid solar, renewables, and grid efficiency days compiled from the UN (International Day of Clean Energy A/RES/77/327), IRENA, IEA, SEforALL, and GOGLA. Observances that are industry-recognized are marked with clear unofficial tags.
  • Global Layer: Official international days designated by the United Nations General Assembly and specialized agencies.
Lunar & Astronomical Dates:
Islamic holidays (Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Eid el-Maulud) are lunar dates calculated based on the Islamic Hijri calendar and dynamically updated following Sultanate / NSCIA moon-sighting announcements.

  • No Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your contact information to any third-party marketing companies.
  • Right to Unsubscribe: Every email alert features a direct 1-click unsubscribe link. You can also manage or terminate your subscription at any time using our Manage Alerts portal.
